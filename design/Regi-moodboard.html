Colour Philosophy
The rule: colour = signal, not style
The UI is predominantly ink on white. The three brand colours appear only when they carry specific meaning — never decoratively. A user should be able to read the colour of an element and understand its function.
Pink
The active state. Appears on the current step, the in-progress regimen, streak milestones, and primary CTAs when action is urgent or celebratory. Never used as background fill in passive UI.
Blue
The informational state. Used for links, secondary actions, and system messages. Appears when something is available but not urgent — evening routine, saved items, help text.
Yellow
The sharing state. Reserved exclusively for the share/send mechanic — the yellow share button, the shared regimen badge, the "sent" confirmation. Yellow means someone else is involved.
Ink + Grey
Everything else. The vast majority of the UI — steps, cards, labels, navigation, body copy — uses the ink and grey scale. Colour earns its appearance by carrying meaning.
